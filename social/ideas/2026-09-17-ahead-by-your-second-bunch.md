# Ahead by your second bunch

The strongest content in the business, and it was sitting in a PDF.

A 2019 independent life cycle assessment found that five years of Floral Image
service carries a carbon footprint **80× lower** than five years of replacing
farmed flowers — 29 kg CO₂e against 227 fresh arrangements.

**The post leads with the counterweight, not the boast.** The same report finds
that one of ours costs *more* to make than one bunch of fresh flowers — 190%
more. The advantage is entirely lifespan, and the breakeven is **two
changeovers**.

Draft direction, three sentences:

> One of ours costs more to make than a bunch of fresh flowers.
> By your second bunch, we're already ahead.
> Over five years it's eighty times less carbon.

**Why this is the version to run:** anyone who checks will find the 190% figure,
because it is in the same report. Saying it first is what makes the 80× credible
instead of suspicious — and an ad that concedes something true is read more
carefully than one that doesn't.

**Hard constraints — read `facts/sustainability.md` in full before drafting:**
- Never "80× more sustainable". It is a carbon figure, over five years, against
  replacing fresh flowers, and all three qualifiers travel with it.
- Date it: "independent life cycle assessment, 2019".
- Never attach the figure to a specific design — sample of five, portfolio of
  over a thousand, and the report says so itself.
- No ethical-sourcing or labour angle in any direction.
- Not an attack on fresh florists. In a city this size that lands badly, and it
  is not what the report says anyway.

**Format:** type tile, Forest treatment, three lines. This one does not need a
photograph.
