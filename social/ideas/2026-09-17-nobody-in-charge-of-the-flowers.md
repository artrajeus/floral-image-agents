# Nobody in the office is quietly in charge of the flowers

In most workplaces one person has silently inherited the vase. They top up the
water, they throw out the dead ones, nobody asked them to and nobody thanks them.

**Why it is good:** it is the sharpest line on the existing website and it names
something true that no competitor's copy names. It is also pure B2B — an office
manager reads it and recognises themselves or their colleague.

**Copy direction:** the line almost works as-is. Kicker + headline on a photo
tile of a reception desk, quiet region on the wall behind.
