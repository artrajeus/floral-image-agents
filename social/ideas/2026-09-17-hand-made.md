# Every one of them is made by hand

Hand made, and materials chosen per flower to match how the real one feels — a
flower that is silk to the touch in life is made in silk.

**Why it is good:** it is the most under-used fact in the business. It moves the
product from "artificial flowers" to "designer object", which is the category the
pricing actually sits in.

**Format:** carousel. One card per idea — hand made / material matched to the
real flower / hundreds of designs / five years.

**Hard constraint:** do not write "silk flowers" as a description of the range.
That is only true of the flowers it is true of, and the category-default guess is
wrong here. See `facts/product.md`.
