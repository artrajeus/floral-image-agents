# Cleaned, restyled, rehomed

Collected arrangements are cleaned and placed with another business or home. Five
years of life out of every stem.

**Why it is parked, not open:** this is either the best sustainability story the
business has or a thing a customer feels slightly odd about, and the difference
is entirely framing. Circular economy works. Anything that reads as second-hand
does not. That is Sam's call, not the generator's.

**Note:** this idea does **not** depend on the blocked 80× figure. The reuse
cycle is a fact we hold; the multiplier is not. Do not merge the two.
