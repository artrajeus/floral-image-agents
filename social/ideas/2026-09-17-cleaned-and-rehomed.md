# A different design every month

Unparked 2026-09-17. Aaron confirmed the framing, and it is not the one this file
was originally written around.

**The customer-facing story is the design change.** We deliver an arrangement; a
month later we swap it for a different one, so every month the room looks
different. Nobody chooses it, nobody maintains it, and it never looks like the
same flowers going stale.

**The circular part sits underneath, not on top.** The collected arrangement goes
back into the van, gets cleaned, and goes on to another business or home. Five
years out of every stem. That is the proof that the sustainability claim rests
on, and it is welcome in the post — as the reason, not the headline.

**Do not use the phrase "the illusion of fresh flowers."** It is Aaron's internal
shorthand and it is a good description of the effect, but "illusion" reads
externally as something being put over the customer, which is the opposite of
what is happening.

**Format:** carousel. Four cards, one design per card, same room — the point
makes itself without a word of argument.

⚠ Needs four catalogue references of genuinely different designs.
