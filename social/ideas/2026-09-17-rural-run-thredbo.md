# The van goes to Thredbo

Once a month the run goes out past Bredbo to Cooma, Jindabyne, Perisher and
Thredbo. One day, one loop, back again.

**Why it is good:** almost nobody else in the category can say it, and it is the
most Canberra-and-region thing in the whole business. It also answers a question
a rural prospect is actually asking — *do you even come out here?*

**The honest constraint that has to be in the post:** rural is one trip a month,
so a new rural client waits until the van is next through. Do not let this
become a speed claim. The post is about reach, not immediacy.

**Format:** photo tile, van in landscape, quiet region in the sky for the
headline. Or a type tile listing the towns, which may be stronger — a list of
fourteen place names is its own graphic.
