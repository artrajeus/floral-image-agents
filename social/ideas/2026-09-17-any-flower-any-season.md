# Peonies in August

The range does not change seasonally — deliberately. Life-like designer flowers
mean you can have any flower whether or not it is in season.

**Why it is good:** it reframes the obvious objection. Someone's first thought
about artificial flowers is "they're not real"; this makes the not-real part the
benefit, without ever arguing with them about it.

**Copy direction:** name a specific flower and a specific month when you cannot
get it. Second person. No superlatives.

⚠ Confirm the named flower is actually in the range before it runs — do not name
a flower we do not make. Needs the catalogue.
