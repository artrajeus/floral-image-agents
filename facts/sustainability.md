# Sustainability — the LCA

The 80× figure is **sourced and unblocked**, with conditions. This file exists
separately from `product.md` because an environmental claim carries more
compliance weight than any other claim the business makes, and it needs its
caveats travelling in the same file as its headline.

## What the study actually is

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Cradle-to-grave Life Cycle Assessment, comparative |
| Geography | Australian market |
| Functional unit | **Decorative service over 5 years** |
| Compared against | Farmed (fresh cut) flower arrangements providing the same service |
| Indicators | Global Warming Potential, Human Health (DALY), Ecosystems (species·yr), Resources (USD) |
| Background data | ecoinvent 3.5, AusLCI, Agribalyse |
| Sample | **5 Floral Image arrangements** |
| Exclusions | Infrastructure, labour, non-decorative functions |

## The findings

| Finding | Figure |
|---|---|
| **Headline** | 5 years of Floral Image service has a carbon footprint **80× lower** than 5 years of farmed flower replacements |
| Floral Image, 5-year service | **29 kg CO₂ eq** (average) |
| Farmed arrangements needed to match 5 years | **227** on average (range 183–261; fresh lifespan 7–10 days) |
| **1:1, single arrangement** | Floral Image is **190% HIGHER** than one farmed arrangement |
| **Breakeven** | **2 changeovers.** Ahead from the second bunch of fresh flowers onward. |
| Reduction, all four indicators | **98%–100%** (GWP 99.7–99.8 · Health 99.7–99.9 · Ecosystems 98.1–98.9 · Resources 98.3–99.0) |

Floral Image's own impact hotspots, per the report: **epoxy resin, manufacturing
electricity, the service vans, and disposal.**

## Approved wording

The claim is about **carbon footprint over five years of service**, compared with
**replacing farmed flowers**. Every one of those qualifiers is load-bearing.

✅ **"Five years of Floral Image is 80× lower carbon than five years of replacing
fresh flowers."**
✅ "Independent life cycle assessment, 2019."
✅ "29 kg of CO₂ over five years. The fresh equivalent takes 227 arrangements."
✅ **"We're ahead by your second bunch."** ← the best line in the whole report

❌ "80× more sustainable" — "sustainable" is unqualified and undefined; the
   number is a carbon number.
❌ "80× better for the environment" — same problem.
❌ "80× greener", "carbon neutral", "zero impact", "eco-friendly", "sustainable
   flowers" — none of these are what the study measured.
❌ Any 80× claim without **both** the 5-year period **and** the fresh-flower
   comparison in the same sentence.

## The counterweight, and why it goes in the post

**On a one-for-one basis, a Floral Image arrangement has a 190% higher footprint
than a single bunch of fresh flowers.** The advantage is entirely from lifespan.

This sits in the same report as the 80×. Anyone who challenges the claim will
find it, so it is better said by us first — and the honest version is a genuinely
better post than the boast:

> One of ours costs more to make than one bunch of fresh flowers. By your second
> bunch we're already ahead. Over five years it's eighty times less carbon.

That is the whole story in three sentences, it is fully substantiated, and it is
much harder to attack than "80× more sustainable".

## ⚠ Limits that must be respected

**1. The study is from 2019.** Seven years old, on ecoinvent 3.5. The PDF carries
2025 branding, but the data and findings are 2019. ACCC guidance expects
substantiation to be current. **Ask head office whether it has been updated or
re-verified since**, and record the answer here. Usable meanwhile, dated
explicitly as "2019" in any post.

**2. Sample of 5, portfolio of 1,000+.** The report flags this itself, rates its
influence **Medium**, and states that extrapolating individual arrangement
results to the entire portfolio "may not be applicable." So: **never attach the
figure to a specific design.** The claim is about the service, in aggregate.

**3. No independent critical review is claimed.** The report names an author and
an internal reviewer. It does not state an ISO 14040/14044 critical review panel.
So: ✅ "independent life cycle assessment" (Edge Environment is independent of
Floral Image). ❌ "ISO-certified", "third-party verified", "audited".

**4. The report contains a modern-slavery risk section.** It finds Chinese
suppliers carry higher social risk than Australian ones — and Floral Image
arrangements are manufactured in China. **No ethical-sourcing, modern-slavery or
labour claim may be made in any direction.** The carbon story is strong and it
stands alone; reaching for an ethics claim on top of it opens a question this
document does not answer favourably.

**5. It is a comparative claim about farmed flowers.** Keep the tone factual.
Fresh florists are not the villain, and a post that reads as an attack on local
florists will land badly in a city this size.
