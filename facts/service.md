# The service

## Refresh rhythm

**Monthly**, for both business and regular home clients.

**And it is not strict.** A refresh may land a couple of days early or a couple
of days late depending on how the run is moving and other factors. This is
normal and it is fine to be relaxed about it in tone — what is not fine is
writing anything that promises a fixed date we do not hold to, such as "the same
day every month" or "like clockwork".

## Coverage

**All of Canberra and the surrounding region.** Named, in Aaron's words,
"including but not limited to":

Queanbeyan · Hall · Murrumbateman · Yass · Crookwell · Gunning · Goulburn ·
Braidwood · Bungendore · Bredbo · Cooma · Jindabyne · Perisher · Thredbo

Anything within that radius is covered.

### The rural runs

Rural areas get **one trip per month**, grouped into single days:

| Day | Towns |
|---|---|
| One day | Yass, Murrumbateman |
| One day | Gunning, Crookwell, Goulburn, Braidwood, Bungendore |
| One day | Bredbo, Cooma, Jindabyne, Perisher, Thredbo |

This is excellent content and almost nobody else in the category can say it. A
van that goes to Thredbo once a month is a story. It is also a constraint that
must be stated honestly in any post that implies speed — see response times.

⚠ Which weekday each rural run falls on is not recorded. Do not state a day.

## Hours

- **Monday to Friday, 9–5.** Business days only.
- **Homes:** one or two days a week run outside business hours, because that is
  when people are home. Alternatively a home client can leave the arrangement
  out the front if there is a safe spot, and the run comes through during the
  day.

## Who turns up

**Sam and Albert** do the refreshes — whoever is on the run that day.

**The public word is "designers".** Recently adopted in advertising. Use it.
Do not use "driver", "delivery guy", "technician", or "rep".

## Response and lead time

- **Enquiries answered within 24 hours** (target).
- First arrangement can often be organised **same day**, or whenever suits the
  client.
- **Except rural** — rural clients wait until the van is next in the area, which
  may be up to a month.

Any post promising speed must not be targeted at, or read as applying to, rural
areas. "Flowers on your desk today" is true in Braddon and false in Cooma.
