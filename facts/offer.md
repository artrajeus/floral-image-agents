# The offer

## Free trial

| Segment | Trial length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Business | **1 week** | |
| Home | **2 weeks** | |

**It is deliberately not strict.** If the run is busy and we do not get back at
the week mark, the client keeps the arrangement longer. This is a real feature of
how the business operates, not slippage, and it is the honest thing to say.

**Approved caption wording:** "a week on us" / "two weeks on us". Do **not**
write "exactly one week", "7 days only", or anything that implies a hard
deadline we do not enforce.

**The six-week win-back offer is resolved — and it is not social content.**
`welcome-back.html` offers six weeks free to lapsed clients. Aaron, 2026-09-17:
it is an idea the boss floated to bring old clients back, shows them the new
range free for six weeks and then sells after, **"just an idea for the moment,
may implement — not something that we will advertise."**

So: it is a direct, one-to-one win-back play, not a public offer. **No post
mentions six weeks**, and the live trial figures for social are 1 week / 2 weeks.
No inconsistency to fix on the site; the page is simply not a social surface.

## Pricing

Corporate regular refresh rates, per week:

| Size | Name | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Small | **Splash of Colour** | **$13/week** |
| Medium | **Conversation Starter** | **$15/week** |
| Large | **Wow Factor** | **$17/week** |

**Approved to state publicly.** Lead form: **"from $13 a week"**.

Note the mismatch between the billing rate and the service rhythm: the rate is
quoted *per week*, the refresh happens *monthly*. Both are true and they are not
in tension, but a caption must not imply a weekly visit. Say "from $13 a week"
for the price and "refreshed every month" for the service; never fuse them into
"fresh flowers every week for $13".

⚠ Home pricing not supplied. No home price appears in any post until it is.

## Terms

- **No contract at all.** Month to month. Cancel any time.
- No card required for the trial.
- If they do not want to continue, we collect the arrangement.

**Approved wording:** "no contract, cancel any time, month to month."

Because the offer is free with a condition attached (we collect if you stop),
the condition is stated in the same breath as the word "free" — never in a
second post, never only in the comments, never only on the landing page.
