Is it really free for six weeks?
Yes. No card, no invoice, nothing that rolls over automatically. We deliver, style and refresh the arrangement for six weeks. At the end you tell us whether to keep them coming or collect them.

Our current range, for a start: premium, life-like, allergy-free pieces that never wilt and need no water or maintenance. And a Canberra team that refreshes them monthly across 600+ workplaces, so the flowers on your desk are never the same for long.
How quickly do the flowers arrive?
We deliver across Canberra and the surrounding region on set run days each week, so you'll usually have flowers within a few business days of signing up. We'll confirm the day with you first.

We ask one question: keep them, or collect them? If you'd like to keep them, we set you up on a monthly plan. If not, we pick them up. No pressure either way, and no hard sell in between.

Plans vary by size and how many arrangements you'd like, so tell us in the notes and we'll come with options. Either way, the six weeks are free, so there's nothing to decide until you've seen them in your space.
Can we choose the style?
Absolutely. Tell us what you'd like in the notes, or what you'd rather avoid, and our team will style to suit your space and your brand.
